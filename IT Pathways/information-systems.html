Bachelor of Science in Information Systems

Designing technology solutions that drive business success

Program Overview

Information Systems is one of the fastest-growing fields today, offering opportunities to explore technologies and develop solutions that enhance businesses. The program prepares students to design, implement, and manage technology-based systems that support organizational goals.

Information Systems program bridges the gap between business needs and technology solutions, equipping students with both technical expertise and business acumen to drive digital transformation in organizations of all sizes.

Key Focus Areas

  • Business Process Analysis and Design
  • Enterprise Systems and Architecture
  • Database Management and Business Intelligence
  • Systems Analysis and Design
  • IT Project Management
  • Digital Transformation Strategies

Career Opportunities

Graduates of our Information Systems program are prepared for diverse roles across various industries including finance, healthcare, retail, government, and technology consulting firms.

  • Computer and Information Research Scientists
  • Software Developers, Quality Assurance Analysts, and Testers
  • Computer Network Architects
  • Information Security Analysts
  • Database Administrators and Architects
  • Computer Systems Analysts
  • Computer Programmers
  • Network and Computer Systems Administrators
  • Computer Support Specialists
  • Web Developers and Digital Designers
