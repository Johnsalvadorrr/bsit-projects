Bachelor of Science in Data Science

Extracting insights from data to drive decisions in the digital era

Program Overview

This program is an interdisciplinary program that is designed to equip its graduates with integrated skill sets spanning mathematics, statistics, machine learning, databases and other branches of computer science with the aim of extracting new knowledge from data in various forms in order to provide actionable insights for decision makers in data-driven industries and sectors.

In this era of big data and digitization, the BSDS program is primarily instigated to address the sustained, ever increasing, demand for data scientists, data-driven thinkers, and business analysts.

Key Focus Areas

  • Statistical Analysis and Modeling
  • Machine Learning and AI Applications
  • Data Visualization and Communication
  • Big Data Technologies and Infrastructure
  • Database Management and Data Warehousing
  • Business Intelligence and Analytics

Career Opportunities

Graduates of our Data Science program are prepared for high-demand roles across various industries including technology, finance, healthcare, retail, and research organizations.

  • Data Scientist
  • Data Engineer
  • Data Analyst
  • Database Administrator
  • Statistician
  • Functional Analyst
  • Business Analyst
  • Predictive Modeler
  • Data Architect
  • Data Governance Specialist
  • Data Visualization Specialist
  • Computational Scientist
  • Data Analyst for Medical/Pharmaceutical Firms
  • Market Analyst
